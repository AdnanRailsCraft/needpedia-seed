WHAT'S MISSING FROM TRIAL 2, AND WHAT ISN'T FINISHED

- rebuild/: step-by-step rebuild instructions now exist as rebuild/
  (README.md is the index; NEXUS.md covers the document server). The
  main site's tested guide is still
  documents/developer-wiki/Needpedia-Test-Site-Package.md; the other
  systems' per-system files are listed as not written in the checklist
  in rebuild/README.md.
- Passwords, keys and tokens: every one in the code was replaced
  with REPLACE_ME, with one known exception: the phone app's
  Firebase configuration (code/phone-app/lib/firebase_options.dart)
  still carries Needpedia's real Firebase API keys. Firebase client
  keys are meant to be public — access is controlled by each
  project's security rules — but replace them with keys for your own
  Firebase project before you ship an app. Make the rest of your own.
- The Nexus server's volume paths are now portable: both
  code/nexus/docker-compose.yml and home-docker-compose.yml use the
  NEXUS_DIR environment variable (default ./Needpedia_Nexus next to the
  settings file) and HOME instead of the original author's desktop
  paths. Bring it up following rebuild/NEXUS.md.
- The Nexus's videos, audio and mixed media: left out. Its images
  are in media/images/.
- The Nexus's volunteer accounts: left out. Make your own.
- Email templates and page pop-up guides from Needpedia's admin
  pages: not saved. Write your own.

THE NETWORK OF NEEDPEDIA SERVERS: A PLAN, PARTLY BUILT
We plan to link Needpedia servers into a global network that
interacts and backs itself up, largely inspired by the fediverse.
How far along it is (checked in the code, 2 October 2026):
- Working: following people on other fediverse servers (Mastodon
  and similar) and showing their posts.
- Built but switched off and untested: sending Needpedia posts out
  to other servers (app/models/post.rb, lines 114 and 115).
- Must be fixed before switching that on: incoming messages are not
  properly checked for who sent them.
- Not built yet: servers backing each other up.

If you get stuck, write down exactly where and why. That
list is the most useful result of a trial.
