Welcome! Needpedia is building something the world has never had: an

open archive of problems and the ideas that could solve them. It's a

small nonprofit with a tiny team, which means two things are true at

once: you will not get lost in a bureaucracy, and your work will

actually matter. Here's how your first week goes.

== DAY 1: SAY HELLO ==

Write to the volunteer coordination inbox (vc-email-five.vercel.app,

or email VC@Needpedia.org). Heads up: this system is PUBLIC — anyone

can read it. That's deliberate; it's how we coordinate (see "How the

Public Email System Works & Why"). If you want to send something

private, like a resume, use Needpedia@gmail.com.

Tell us three things:

1. What you're good at (or want to get good at),

2. What you're interested in,

3. Roughly how much time you have.

That's it. No forms, no interviews. Adele — our volunteer AI — reads

the inbox and can answer questions any time, including "what does

Needpedia need help with right now?"

== DAY 1-2: FIND YOUR GROUP ==

Volunteer work is organized through task cards in group accounts.

There are three main doors:

- DEVELOPERS (improving Needpedia itself):

https://needpedia.org/groups/10

Note: you no longer need Docker to run Needpedia locally — GitHub

Codespaces lets you run everything on GitHub. Ask Adele for the dev

onboarding guide.

- AI LEARNERS (learn AI hands-on by improving Needpedia's AIs —

Florence, Lotte, and Adele):

https://needpedia.org/groups/12

- EVERYONE ELSE (writing, design, outreach, translation, research,

media, and more):

https://needpedia.org/groups/17

Browse the task cards. Each has skill tags and a description. If

nothing fits, say so in the inbox — tell us what you're good at and

we'll either find something or invite you to propose your own task.

== DAY 2-5: DO ONE SMALL THING ==

Pick something small enough to finish in one or two sittings. Seriously

— small. A finished small task beats an abandoned big one, it teaches

you how we work, and it gets your name into Needpedia's contribution

record (/impact) immediately. When you finish (or get stuck!), post an

update in the public inbox. Stuck is normal; unanswered is not — if

you're stuck, someone will help.

== ANYTIME: THE EXTRAS ==

- BI-WEEKLY MEETINGS: totally optional video calls. We work

internationally, so our scheduler handles any time zone; meeting

links come from VC@Needpedia.org.

- THE NEXUS (nexus.needpedia.org): our resource hub — articles,

glossary, media, and tools. Volunteers with the password also get

AI image/text studios and a drag-and-drop file upload that preserves

folder structure (email attachments work too).

- LOTTE: once you have a Needpedia account, Lotte (the on-site AI) can

find, create, and edit wiki posts for you — you never have to learn

the interface.

== THINGS WORTH KNOWING EARLY ==

- Needpedia is young. Some features are experimental, the user base is

small, and you're building the ground floor. That's the appeal.

- We're nonpartisan, pro civil society, pro human rights, always.

- Life happens; if you need to drop a task, just say so in the inbox

so it can be picked up. No guilt. Silence is the only thing that

hurts us.

- Conduct concerns or anything you want to report anonymously:

Needpedia@gmail.com.

== YOUR FIRST WEEK, IN SHORT ==

1. Introduce yourself in the public inbox.

2. Pick a group (10 = dev, 12 = AI, 17 = everything else).

3. Finish one small task.

4. Tell us how it went.

Do those four things and you'll know more about how Needpedia works

than any document could teach you. Welcome aboard.
