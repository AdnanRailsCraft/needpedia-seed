Needpedia Use Cases

1. Sharing an Idea or Proposal

The most fundamental action. Any user can post a new idea or solution to a problem listed on the platform.

2. Finding Solutions to a Problem

Users can search for specific problems, like "homelessness in Portland" or "back pain," and instantly see a ranked list of all proposed solutions and related discussions.

3. Finding Communities Who Need Your Skills

Users can search for 'task cards' posted by communities who need help, using skill tags such as "writing," "coding," or "construction." This makes it easier than ever before to find something you can do to help people in your area or beyond.

4. Constructive Social Media

Needpedia's social media section is populated with people who are working on projects to make the world a better place, so that itself should make it an interesting place to be; but because we can use Needpedia to improve Needpedia, we can continuously improve Needpedia's social media section as well. In time users will be able to choose and help create the algorithms controlling what they see and hear. The possibilities are practically endless.

5. Dropping a Question Token

Using the Token System, a user can drop a question token directly onto a specific word or statement within any idea post to ask for clarification or more information.

6. Debate Tokens

A user engages with a debate token to see the arguments for and against an idea. They can individually rate each argument on both sides, contributing to a precise understanding of what everyone's concerned about in particular.

7. Getting Help via the Time Bank

A user posts a task they need help with, offers time credits, and connects with others in the network to exchange services (e.g., "I'll fix your website for 5 credits, which I can then use to get help promoting my own project").

8. Bartering Goods/Services

A user lists items or services they Have or Want in the dedicated section, enabling people to waste less and help each other more.

9. Tracking an Idea

Users can subscribe to ideas they care about, creating a personalized feed of updates and ensuring they stay engaged in the evolution of solutions that matter to them.

10. Learning from Experts on Expert Layers

Everyday people are free to learn what experts are saying about ideas by visiting the expert layers added to them.

11. Browse the Fediverse, Reddit, and Other Sites

To help our users find content from organizers we've created features that enable them to access the fediverse and other sites where a lot of organizing happens.

12. Finding Community

On Needpedia, finding communities of people who share a vision and need your help is as easy as looking for ideas in your area that resonate with you. The people working on those ideas need help and have listed their objectives and current efforts where it’s easy to see and interact with.

13. Interdisciplinary Coordination (Using Needpedia's Layers System)

The Layers System lets people add dedicated layers to any idea post, which helps break down massive interdisciplinary ideas into the various fields they involve. This structure is built for citizen-expert collaboration but also works great for expert-expert coordination.

14. Pressure Campaigns

Once a city or other community identifies the solutions they want, and experts have helped them assess which ones are most feasible, any politician, business, or other public entity which refuses to comply with those solutions, will be forced to tell the public that they know the solutions exist, but they simply choose to ignore them. Which makes running pressure campaigns far more potent, targeted, and strategic.

15. Open Civic Operations – Creation and Governance of Public Entities

A community could use Needpedia to brainstorm ideas for an abandoned space, decide to create a community kitchen, and then manage all aspects of the project on the platform—from recruiting volunteers and making agreements to handling problems as they arise.

16. Conflict Resolution

Needpedia's Layer System can be used to facilitate conflict resolution. If an idea is controversial, simply create a layer for Nonviolent Communication (NVC) wherein people are expected to communicate using expressions of needs, direct observations, and requests.

17. Identity Representation

If someone has an idea for something public like a homeless shelter, then women, people with disabilities, and people with experience actually relying on shelters should all get their own layers to discuss the idea amongst themselves. Then provide their input on the public layer where everyone else will see it.

18. Dispelling Myths, Misinformation, and Fallacies

On Needpedia, all falsehoods are sitting ducks because you can drop a Note Token after any statement you want, which lets you speak truth to power, and do handy things like link people to sources or even entire documentaries. If someone wants to hide what's happening to people, Note Tokens let you show everyone exactly who those people are, and exactly what they had to say. They're simple but powerful.

19. Platform Self-Improvement

We can use Needpedia to improve Needpedia, leveraging the entire community and its tools to iteratively and endlessly improve upon the system.

20. Emergency Response Coordination

The same structured framework for solving everyday problems can also be applied to emergencies. This provides a simple, organized, and highly effective process for rapid response and coordination during a crisis. The worse things are the more valuable it becomes being able to quickly list what the problems are, and all of their potential solutions.

21. Preventing Extremism

In addition to helping people organize against fascist movements by improving their ability to share and organize around ideas, Needpedia is built to oppose fascism in countless other ways as well:

  • Where fascists work to demonize social demographics, our Layer System gives them all a voice. We actually welcome their various perspectives and insights.

  • Where fascists work to erode the very concept of truth, our Token System enables everyone to point out falsehoods and ridicule them in an extremely public fashion. Right where all the decisions are being made, where the truth can have the greatest effect.

  • Where fascism seeks to dehumanize entire populations, NVC layers on Needpedia will rehumanize not only the people in those conversations, but as people increasingly learn to use NVC in their own lives, it will begin rehumanizing the entire world. Because NVC makes you realize problems are just situations where people's needs aren't being met, good and evil are just concepts humans created.

  • Where fascism tricks people into thinking only violence and authority can solve our problems, every single thing we accomplish together will prove they are wrong. And will illuminate the nature of true power.

  • And where fascists work to convince us there's nothing that can be done, and nothing beyond the world they create, we will see the dreams of our neighbours, and in helping them we will become good friends. We will become family.

With an entire world of communities equipped and empowered in all these different ways, combined, violent bigoted movements like those of fascists will no longer stand a chance.
