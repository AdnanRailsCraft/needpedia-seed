Doing the impossible

Anthony Brasher


How can we possibly live in peace after so many of us have learned to hate each other so much?


By rethinking evolutionary processes.


Case in point: Dan is a conservative father, and someone’s proposed a really cool idea for improving the playground at his son’s school. He likes it too and shows up to help. On Needpedia, there is no republican or democrat, there are only ideas and the people who like them. Dan heard about an idea he liked and started showing up.


First online, on the idea post for the playground re-design. He scrolls down to the objectives section inside the idea post, and notices that they need a piece of equipment he has, so he comments on the related objective that he’s happy to show up and even operate it for them.


The moment Dan knocked out that objective everyone else following the idea got an update through the site. Naturally everyone’s excited to hear another objective’s met and a few are reminded that they’ve been meaning to post about this idea on their own social media to help promote it. The news created by Dan gives those posts an extra element of excitement and a few friends of theirs pick up on that and start getting inspiration too.


Eventually the final objective is completed, which in this case was covering the cost of materials, and the community sets a date to make it all happen. The school approves a staging ground for the materials to get delivered and accessed when it’s time, and the community talks extensively in the objectives and comment section of the idea post about logistics and planning.


The day comes and Dan arrives with his equipment, he sees a crowd of other parents, and notices a few are black or brown skinned, and that a few look queer as well. are


Being conservative, he knows they might hate guys like him and puts up his guard, and tries focusing on the project. He did invest a bit into the cost of materials too and honestly does want this project to be a success.


The principle is serving as the project coordinator, greets Dan warmly and lets him know the site’s marked and ready for him to get started whenever he’s ready. Dan fires up his machine and feels a brief moment of anxiety noticing a bunch of parents who don’t have anything to do yet are watching him. Fortunately he also knows he’s damn good at what he does and starts focusing on that, and before he knows it, all the spots that were marked for drilling are turned into holes and it’s time for the next stage to begin. He notices one of the parents helping transport materials looks like a dyke, but she’s extremely good at coordinating with the other people helping carry boards. She asks if they’re ready to lift, counts down from 3, and moves at the same pace they do. Dan’s worked in construction long enough to know how quickly people can get hurt when they don’t bother communicating or doing other things like that, and can’t help but feel a sense of respect. But there’s still no telling if she would have any respect for him. If Dan was only planning to stay as long as it took him to operate his equipment that might be all the social progress we could hope to see that day, but because this new playground idea’s super cool, it’s also kind of elaborate and will need lots of help, so Dan’s already decided to make it a day.


Also everyone else is really excited, and friendly. They’re in high spirits, and a few even begin to wonder what it was like for the people who build the playgrounds they used to enjoy as kids. They feel a deeps sense of agency which they do not even have the words to fully grasp themselves yet, let alone communicate. A sense of agency and connection, a place in the world and the fabric of human life. They are not mere consumers, they’re creators. And a small part of history.


While Dan’s helping start the foundation, he notices one of the people bringing him supplies is the dyke he saw earlier, she was already in a good mood but absolutely lights up seeing the drill guy. She says “Heyy, nice work with the drill! Did you used to work in landscaping or construction? That definitely wasn’t your first rodeo.”

He says ‘thanks’ and they start having an interesting talk about working in construction as apparently Karry, (the dyke), does too.


And that stuck with Dan, later as he heard people talk about queer people. Karry didn’t seem evil or anything, actually he gets the sense she’s probably a really good mom. Irregardless of whether she likes women too. It even dawns on him that the idea she can’t be a good parent because she likes women is truly just as absurd as someone saying he can’t be a good parent because he likes women.


This exchange between Dan and Karry, constitutes a single moment of meaningful progress. The destination is a world where we all live in safety, and to create that we must look the hatred in our own hearts and minds right in their ugly faces and recognize that we will not be sacrificing our futures, and the futures of our family, and all those we love and hold dear in this world, to the putrid beast that is hatred.


Hate is a feeling and one cannot choose what they feel, thus it may seem the world is doomed.


But nature is miraculous, life itself a vast and endless enigma. If we are to give in to anything let it be hope, I’m not asking you to love everyone. Love is a feeling and one cannot choose what they feel.


Instead I have some good news about making it easier. The only way Karry was ever gonna meet or end up liking Dan, was if something brought them together. Particularly something positive, and mutually beneficial.


With an archive for all ideas for all problems with all things, such as Needpedia, these interactions increasingly become absolutely unavoidable. Because each successful idea created its own little tribe. And the same Darwinistic forces that shaped our psychology absolutely will re-manifest here. Especially for ideas that play out over longer periods of time. For instance, studies have shown that if two people know each other for longer periods of time and neither one has done anything especially bad, that they will most likely develop an affinity. Affinity between members of a pre-historic tribe increased the likelihood of survival enormously, not only by decreasing the odds of them killing each other, but also because it increases the likelihood of coordination.


That’s how humans dominated lions.


It’s how we survived before and I sincerely believe it’s how we can survive again. Things like military industrial complexes are indeed infinitely more fearsome than lions, but when you shift your focus to process, things start to look very different.


The current process we use to make decisions, is basically just to let a bunch of rich assholes do battle politically to see who gets to rule over us for the next few years. If that sounds primitive and ineffective, that’s because it is.


We to face that, but we don’t have to take it head on. I now see the path to a better world clearly, each step is small and highly feasible.


Where before I addressed the impossibility of changing the human heart, en masse, I will now address the equally miraculous feat of changing the political system around us.


It starts extremely small. A child could have an idea for improving their school. They share it with friends and suddenly there’s a100 more kids who know how to use the archive for sharing and collaborating on ideas.


Additionally, Needpedia lets you share ideas for anything, even conceptual things, like social movements. This means we get to vote on tons of things that the gov absolutely cannot control. And if we manage to make decisions we like, and find ourselves liking the platform, those small victories will grow into larger and larger community efforts. Needpedia’s already designed to collaborate with experts in multiple fields, and with people who are experts in lived experiences such as navigating life as a person of color, as a woman, and/or as someone with a disability. So that when people share ideas for public things, the community gets to hear from everybody. Needpedia’s already designed for high level mass participation, and we can start sharing ideas for entire countries from day 1.


More importantly, we can begin showcasing the best ideas on Earth from day one. Often the solutions to our problems already exist, they’re just being ignored. Or mischaracterized.


So we start by showcasing them, and ridiculing representatives who fail to represent us. But you have to understand that the real game changer is that the public begins to realize there’s a better option. One that’s working right now and absolutely can be scaled up, and even used to affect change in the current system.


With massive citizen-expert coalitions that have already discussed every angle of an idea, any “representative” who chooses to keep ignoring it, will not only look terrible, (exactly as they should, decisions like these make people like us, die), each time it will create news reports and other publicity. Bad press for them, good press for us. People will begin to realize that life’s about opportunity and that with an archive for all our dreams for a better world, with every conceivable subject in human existence, we can begin meeting people who excite us, and really appreciate us. And even possibly need us. With these tools we may transition an entire species from that of consumer to creator. Now that it’s built all it needs is a community of people like you, who would take the time to think about things like this, and hopefully care about something in this world enough to try making a difference. May the future belong to you.



“But that’s too simple” you’re still asking, and of course the answer to world peace isn’t going to fit into a short essay like this but I can tell you this, we have more at our disposal than Darwinistic traits. The study of the human mind, formalized into modern psychology, has become useless to most people. But in NonViolent Communication the ability to quickly and easily begin bypassing countless problems appears. In NVC there is no ‘right or wrong’, problems are nothing more than situations where people’s needs aren’t being met. And the same Layer System that lets people hear from experts and others, can also be used for NVC.


Not only that, but because NVC has extremely clear guidelines, AI can be used to only allow statements that comply with NVC, in NVC layers. This creates something truly special, combined with the other features of Needpedia like the ability to find ideas using tags or keywords for things that excite you, people can spend entire days discussing and collaborating on ideas that excite them without every needing to worry if others are going to insult or belittle them. Nomatter how controversial an idea is, as long as it’s not banned and we can host it, you can work on it without insults or insinuation. NVC is also incredibly good at solving problems quickly because it’s super simple and straightforward. Potential drama aside, it can be used to get a lot done.


And it has the ability to ‘bleed out into the real world’. A father who learns how to use NVC on Needpedia, is a father who also knows how to use NVC with his kids. And spouse. That is a less violent father, who himself will feel less isolated from his own family due to there being less violence. Countless fathers work their butts off and feel like no one really appreciates them and often there’s a literal fear of them, psychologically or otherwise, which no amount of money can cure.


How beautiful would it be if an entire generation of angry fathers realized how things got this way, with the profitability of their hate, and suddenly rejected all of it. It’s only natural to want love, in fact it is a matter of survival so never take it lightly.


But we were talking about seriously working towards world peace. How does NVC apply to that? All the solutions that can really change the world, are going to end up with things like political attacks and demonization. NVC layers have the power to cut right through all of that. There’s additional tools Needpedia already has like the Token System which can also be used to publicly dismantle things like fallacies, myths, and misinformation.


By the way these are only the features that exist now. Needpedia isn’t like other systems where they’re mostly set in stone, it’s a place to share ideas for improving anything including Needpedia, and that means we have no idea how many more good ideas are yet to come. We have no idea how many new tools people will think of for empowering people to improve the world. And when problems come up with Needpedia itself, we can also use Needpedia to list them publicly, to crowdsource solutions to our own problems as they arise.


Is that all? Needpedia, NVC, and hope?


No, I’m just focusing on naming new elements of a larger system, to really understand change you have to understand how these will play off of each other. For instance, Needpedia can be used by unions to gather ideas from their members more effectively, and that could lead to more effective organizing in countless different ways. Those all create ripple effects that enable more people to not only see an archive for ideas now exists, but that it’s possible for them to share their own dreams and maybe even call up their friends or coworkers, which then begets more ripple effects. What you’re looking at here is a powerful positive feedback loop, one we hope is powerful enough to eventually change everything and everyone, inside and out.
