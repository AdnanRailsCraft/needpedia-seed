== WHAT NEEDPEDIA IS ==

Needpedia (Needpedia.org) is a free, nonprofit platform: an open

archive of problems and the ideas that could solve them. The world has

never had an official archive for people's ideas — Needpedia is being

built to be exactly that. Think "Wikipedia, but for ideas": anyone can

post a Subject (any topic — a city, a school, a social movement, a

fandom, a language), list Problems under that subject, and list Ideas

under each problem, so everyone can see all the options side by side.

Needpedia is both a wiki and a social network. The wiki section holds

subjects, problems, and ideas. The social media section is for

discussion, updates, and community building — posts there are visible

to everyone by default; you don't need to be friends with someone to

see what they share.

Serious and non-serious content are both welcome. Global crises, local

playgrounds, fan fiction, and constructed languages all belong here,

as long as content stays within the Terms of Service.

== HOW POSTING WORKS ==

1. SEARCH FIRST. Always check whether a post already exists before

creating one. Duplicates fragment collaboration.

2. THE CHAIN: Subject post → Problem post → Idea post. Create missing

parents before children.

3. TAXONOMY: Use "Earth" as the subject for problems affecting the

whole planet or humanity as a whole. Use regional subcategories

(e.g., Earth > Africa) for regional issues, and thematic subjects

(Culture, Technology, Health, Environment, Politics) or specific

subjects (Poverty, Education, a city, a school) for narrower scopes.

4. Use clear, descriptive titles ("Eradicating Extreme Poverty

Globally") and tags so others can find your post.

== KEY FEATURES ==

- LAYER SYSTEM: Add layers to posts so specific groups get dedicated

space — experts, people with relevant lived experience (e.g., people

who've experienced homelessness discussing a shelter idea), and NVC

(Nonviolent Communication) layers for conflict resolution. Layers

work for serious and fun content alike. Find the "Layer" button at

the upper right of a post.

- TOKEN SYSTEM: Drop tokens directly into a post's text (via the Edit

button) at the exact word or claim they concern: Question tokens ask

for clarification, Note tokens add sources or corrections, Debate tokens host

structured arguments people can rate individually. No drag-and-drop

yet.

- LOL VOTES: A separate vote so funny-but-bad ideas can be enjoyed

without being ranked as good ideas.

- OBJECTIVES & TASK CARDS: Idea posts contain objectives — concrete

steps toward making the idea real. Task cards carry skill tags

(coding, writing, construction...) and checkback dates so helpers

can find work that fits them. Followers of a post get notified as

objectives are completed.

- TIME BANK: Exchange help for time credits ("I'll fix your website

for 5 credits, then spend them getting help with my own project").

- HAVE/WANT: Profile sections for listing goods and services you have

or want — like a digital garage sale. Requires two-factor

authentication (2FA) to use.

- MAP ("GEOMAXXING"): Share ideas tied to physical locations, so

spatial ideas for real places are easy to see and support.

- GROUP ACCOUNTS: Organizations and teams can operate group accounts

and post tasks under them.

- /IMPACT: Contributions are credited, GitHub-style, so people who

help ideas become reality build visible track records and networks

of trust.

- SEARCH: A dedicated search page with extensive filters.

- FEDIVERSE & FEDERATION: Needpedia can share wiki content between

Needpedia instances and interact with the fediverse. These features

are EXPERIMENTAL and lightly tested — set expectations accordingly.

== THE THREE AI ASSISTANTS ==

All three are named after librarians who resisted fascism.

- FLORENCE greets visitors before they log in, answers questions, and

helps people decide whether to create an account.

- LOTTE is the main assistant for logged-in users. She can search,

create, and edit wiki posts on your behalf via API — you never have

to learn the interface. She works in many languages and can explain

anything on the site, including in age-appropriate ways.

- ADELE handles volunteer coordination in Needpedia's public email

system, where all messages are publicly viewable for coordination,

situational awareness, and transparency.

Florence and Lotte each offer a "full" mode and an "eco" mode that

uses over 10x less energy.

== VALUES ==

Needpedia is nonpartisan: it endorses no parties or candidates, and

welcomes ideas from across the political spectrum within the ToS. It

is unambiguously pro civil society and pro human rights. The ToS

prohibits harassment, promotion of violence, and dehumanizing content.

Users can flag content that violates community standards.
Needpedia's AI assistants do not comment on political figures or bodies.

== TRANSPARENCY & SAFETY ==

- Conversations with Needpedia's AI assistants are recorded and

reviewed by the team when possible, to improve service and enforce

the ToS. Content on the site is likewise reviewable.

- Votes are transparent. If you are not certain it's safe for others

to see your vote, don't vote — and contact Needpedia immediately.

- Never share passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal

information with the AI assistants; they will never ask for them.

- Needpedia handles complex, sometimes mature topics and is not

designed for children under 13. Young users are encouraged to

involve an adult; Lotte can act as an age-appropriate guide,

explaining ideas in age-appropriate ways.

- Needpedia is not a crisis service. Anyone in crisis should contact

local emergency services or a crisis line.

== CURRENT STATUS (honesty section) ==

Needpedia is young and growing. The user base is small, some features

are experimental, and the team is tiny. Never overpromise: don't imply

large audiences, guaranteed expert review, or mature federation. What

Needpedia offers today is working infrastructure and a community you

can help build from the ground floor.
