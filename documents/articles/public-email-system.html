HOW THE PUBLIC EMAIL SYSTEM WORKS & WHY

Report conflicts to Needpedia@gmail.com.

== WHAT IT IS ==

Needpedia's volunteer coordination runs through a custom public email

system at vc-email-five.vercel.app. Anyone — volunteer, visitor,

researcher, curious stranger — can open the inbox in guest mode and

read every message. The Volunteer Coordinator (VC) sends mail as

VC@Needpedia.org, and Adele, our volunteer-coordination AI, reads

everything in the system and helps anyone who writes in.

== WHY IT'S PUBLIC: COORDINATION FIRST ==

The main reason is not ideology — it's situational awareness.

In most organizations, coordination knowledge lives in private inboxes

and DMs. When a new volunteer joins, nobody can show them what's

happening; when someone leaves, their knowledge leaves with them; when

two people work on related things, they may never find out. Every

private channel is a small silo, and silos multiply as you grow.

A public inbox inverts all of that:

- Any volunteer can see who is working on what, what's stuck, and

what just got finished — without asking anyone.

- Adele can see it too, which means any volunteer can simply ask her

"what's going on?" or "who's handling X?" and get a real answer.

- Answers only need to be written once. Every reply becomes

documentation the next person can find.

- Handoffs, absences, and scaling stop being emergencies, because the

record of the work is the workspace itself.

This is how a tiny organization gets the coordination capacity of a

much bigger one, and it's designed to keep working as Needpedia grows.

== WHY IT'S PUBLIC: TRUST SECOND ==

Transparency is the bonus. Donors, partner organizations, and future

volunteers don't have to take our word for how we operate — they can

read it. A nonprofit whose coordination happens in the open has very

little room to hide bad behavior, and very little need to be trusted

on faith.

== WHAT THIS MEANS FOR YOU ==

- Write every message knowing anyone can read it, now or years from

now. Clear, kind, and specific beats clever.

- DO use the public system for: task questions, progress updates,

coordination, introductions, ideas, feedback, scheduling.

- DO NOT use it for: anything private. No home addresses, health

information, employment disputes, resumes you'd rather keep private,

or anything about other people they haven't made public themselves.

- For private matters, resumes, or anything sensitive: email

Needpedia@gmail.com instead. That address also accepts ANONYMOUS

reports — including reports about volunteer or coordinator conduct.

- Adele will remind you of this if a conversation drifts toward

personal territory. She never asks for passwords, payment details,

or sensitive personal data; nobody legitimate at Needpedia will.

== WHO'S READING ==

Realistically: the VC, Adele, other volunteers, and occasionally

curious visitors. Messages are part of Needpedia's operational record

and are reviewed by the team when possible, like all activity on

Needpedia's platforms.

== QUICK START ==

1. Open vc-email-five.vercel.app in guest mode and skim recent

threads — that's the current state of the organization.

2. Write in and introduce yourself. Adele or the VC will take it from

there. (New volunteer? Read "Your First Week as a Volunteer.")
