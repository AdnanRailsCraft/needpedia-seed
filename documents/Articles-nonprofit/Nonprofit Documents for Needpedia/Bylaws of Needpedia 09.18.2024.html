Bylaws of Needpedia

Effective Date: 09.18.2024

Article I: Name

The name of the organization is Needpedia.

Article II: Purpose

Needpedia’s mission is to create the world’s best platform for sharing and collaborating on ideas to solve global challenges, with a focus on empowering communities through open access to solutions and the involvement of experts.

Article III: Board of Directors

  • Composition:
    The Board of Directors shall consist of three members:

    • President: Anthony Brasher

    • Treasurer: Joey Bogalez

    • Secretary: Leah Matheson
      Additional board members may be added with an amendment to the bylaws.

  • Terms:
    Directors serve two-year terms, with no term limits. Terms may be renewed indefinitely unless otherwise amended.

  • Responsibilities:

    • The President oversees general management and has veto power (see "Veto Power" below).

    • The Treasurer manages financial decisions and ensures proper oversight of the organization's funds.

    • The Secretary ensures all records are properly kept, meetings are documented, and organizational compliance is maintained.

  • Veto Power:
    The President has the authority to veto decisions made by voting members to protect the organization from potential abuse or malicious activities, such as a coordinated effort to undermine its governance.

    • The President may exercise this veto power once per month without consulting the other board members or the community of users.

    • Any additional vetoes within a month require consultation with both the board and the community of users.

    • A written explanation must be provided for any veto decision, submitted to the other board members and made publicly available to the community for transparency and accountability.

  • Meetings:
    Formal board meetings are optional but may be held as needed. The President is the primary working member of the board, with the Treasurer and Secretary acting in supportive roles to help navigate paperwork and compliance matters.
    Although meetings are not mandatory, it is strongly recommended that at least one meeting be held per quarter to review organizational progress, financial status, and any necessary actions.

  • Decision-Making & Quorum:
    A quorum for decision-making shall consist of one-third of the board (i.e., the relevant officer for the decision in question). Financial decisions shall be made by the Treasurer but must be approved by the President if the decision exceeds $1,000 or extends beyond normal operations. All financial decisions must be recorded and published for public review.

  • Amendments:
    Amendments to these bylaws may be proposed as idea posts on Needpedia. Amendments must gain at least twice as much voter support as any competing idea post.
    Proposed amendments will be reviewed through a public and transparent process, in which Needpedia may request the input of other respected nonprofit organizations. These organizations will be invited to conduct polls within their communities to gather insights and feedback. The results of these polls, along with feedback from Needpedia's user base, will be made publicly available to ensure transparency and prevent any external manipulation.

Article IV: Conflict of Interest

Any board member or officer with a potential conflict of interest must disclose the conflict and recuse themselves from any decision-making process in which the conflict exists. This policy is designed to maintain the integrity of Needpedia’s operations and ensure that decisions are made in the best interests of the organization and its community.

Article V: Officer Compensation

Officers may be compensated for their work, but compensation must be:

  • Limited to an amount comparable to market rates for similar work in other nonprofit organizations.

  • No greater than the average market rate for similar positions.

  • Reviewed and approved by the board before being implemented.

  • Compensation is anticipated to be modest, likely only a few thousand dollars per month at most.

Article VI: Succession Planning

In the event of the resignation, incapacity, or death of any board member, the following procedures will be followed:

  • President: If the President is unable to fulfill their duties, the Secretary will serve as acting President until a new President is appointed. The board will immediately convene to identify a suitable replacement. A special election may be held within the community to identify the best candidate for this role if no clear successor is in place.

  • Treasurer: If the Treasurer is unable to continue, the President will oversee financial matters until a new Treasurer is appointed. The board will appoint a replacement Treasurer based on qualifications and community feedback.

  • Secretary: If the Secretary cannot continue in their role, the President will appoint a temporary Secretary to ensure organizational records are maintained until a permanent replacement is found.

In each case, the community of Needpedia users may be consulted in an open and transparent process to provide feedback and help ensure that the best candidates are chosen for leadership roles.

Article VII: Fiscal Year

The fiscal year of Needpedia shall begin on January 1 and end on December 31.

Article VIII: Dissolution

In the event of dissolution, any remaining assets shall be distributed to a nonprofit organization that aligns with Needpedia’s mission. The Center for Nonviolent Communication (CNVC) is designated as the beneficiary.
