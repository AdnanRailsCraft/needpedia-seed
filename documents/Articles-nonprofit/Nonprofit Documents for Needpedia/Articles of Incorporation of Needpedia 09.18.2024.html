Articles of Incorporation of Needpedia

Effective Date: 09.18.2024

Article I: Name

The name of the corporation is Needpedia.

Article II: Purpose

Needpedia is a nonprofit organization incorporated in the state of Oregon. Its mission is to create the world's best platform for sharing ideas and collaborating on solutions to global challenges. The organization operates exclusively for charitable and educational purposes under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Article III: Registered Agent and Address

The registered agent for Needpedia is Oregon Registered Agent LLC, with an address at 5305 River Rd. N, Keizer, OR 97303. The corporation’s mailing address is 2000 NE 42nd Ave PMB 1034, Portland, OR 97213.

Article IV: No Private Inurement

No part of the net earnings of the corporation shall inure to the benefit of, or be distributable to, its members, directors, officers, or other private persons, except that the corporation is authorized and empowered to pay reasonable compensation for services rendered and to make payments and distributions in furtherance of the purposes set forth in Article II.

Compensation for services rendered must not exceed the fair market value for similar services provided by individuals in comparable positions at other nonprofit organizations.

Article V: Powers and Restrictions

Needpedia shall have all the powers and authorities granted to nonprofit organizations under Oregon law, but the corporation shall not engage in any activities or exercise any powers that are not in furtherance of its charitable and educational purposes under section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

Needpedia will refrain from engaging in any political campaign activity, either directly or indirectly, including endorsing or opposing candidates for public office. The corporation may engage in educational advocacy activities within the limits allowed by law but will not devote more than an insubstantial part of its activities to attempting to influence legislation.

Article VI: Dissolution Clause

In the event of dissolution, the remaining assets of Needpedia shall be distributed to the Center for Nonviolent Communication (CNVC) or another 501(c)(3) organization that shares similar values and mission, as determined by the Board of Directors. The distribution of assets shall be in accordance with section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code and other applicable laws governing charitable organizations.

Article VII: Conflict of Interest

No member of the Board of Directors or officer of the corporation shall participate in any decision-making process in which they have a personal or financial interest. All potential conflicts of interest must be disclosed and addressed according to Needpedia's Conflict of Interest Policy.

Article VIII: Amendments

These Articles of Incorporation may be amended in the manner prescribed by Needpedia’s bylaws, provided that any amendments comply with section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code and applicable Oregon laws.
