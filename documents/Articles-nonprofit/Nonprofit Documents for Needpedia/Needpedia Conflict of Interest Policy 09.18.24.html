1. Needpedia Conflict of Interest Policy

        Article I: Purpose

The purpose of this Conflict of Interest Policy is to protect the interests of Needpedia, a nonprofit organization, when it is contemplating entering into a transaction or arrangement that might benefit the private interest of a board member, officer, or other person in a position of authority within the organization. This policy is intended to ensure that all decisions made by the board of directors are in the best interest of Needpedia and avoid any potential for personal gain at the organization's expense.

Article II: Definitions

  1. Interested Person
    Anyone on the board or in a position of authority who might have something to gain financially from a decision or arrangement made by Needpedia is considered an "interested person."

  2. Financial Interest
    A person has a financial interest if they, or their close connections (like family or business partners), could benefit from:

    • Owning or investing in a company that does business with Needpedia.

    • Getting paid by Needpedia or any company Needpedia is dealing with.

    • Negotiating a deal or contract with Needpedia that could benefit them financially.

Important Notes:

    • No Corporate Influence: Board members can’t take money, gifts, or perks from corporations or government agencies. If they do, they have to immediately disclose it publicly and may face a vote from the community on whether they should stay on the board.

    • No Golden Parachutes: No special severance deals or perks when leaving the board.

Article III: Procedures

  1. Duty to Disclose
    If anyone on the board has a potential conflict of interest, they need to tell the rest of the board right away and make it public on Needpedia so the community knows.

  2. Handling Conflicts of Interest

    • If there’s a potential conflict, the person with the conflict can explain their side but must step out of the discussion and any vote on the matter.

    • The board (and the community) will decide if the deal or arrangement is fair and in Needpedia’s best interest.

    • Any financial decision over $1000 that involves a board member or their position at Needpedia requires approval from both the president and treasurer, and must be shared with the community.

  3. Violations of the Conflict of Interest Policy

    • If someone doesn’t disclose a conflict and the board finds out, the board can take action. If the person refuses to comply after three official warnings from the president, an emergency session will be held to decide if someone else should take their position.

Article IV: Reporting and Transparency
Any deals, partnerships, or transactions (especially with corporations or government agencies) will be made public on Needpedia, including all discussions and votes on the matter. This keeps everything open and transparent to the community.

Article V: Whistleblower Protections
Anyone who reports a conflict of interest or unethical behavior will not face retaliation. Claims from whistleblowers will be posted publicly to Needpedia’s social media section by the president using the tag "Whistleblower." Retaliation could result in dismissal.
