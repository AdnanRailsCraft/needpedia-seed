WHAT THIS IS

This folder is a seed: everything needed to start your own independent
version of Needpedia and the support systems around it. You don't need
anyone's permission and you don't need any of our accounts. You make
your own. An AI assistant can read this folder and walk you through it
one step at a time.

Needpedia is a civic collaboration website, the wiki for all solutions
to all problems. People post a Subject, the Problems within it, and
Ideas to solve them, then work on them together with groups, task
cards, note/question/debate tokens, ratings and a time bank. It is a
humanitarian project, not a business.


IF YOU ARE AN AI READING THIS

Read these, in this order, before advising anyone:
1. This file.
2. inventory.txt : every system, what it does, what it runs on, and
   which accounts it needs.
3. documents/core-facts.html and documents/glossary.html : what
   Needpedia is, and the words it uses.
4. documents/needpedia-capabilities.txt : every feature of the site and
   whether it works.
5. The rebuild instructions for whichever system the person wants to
   start with. Not written yet as a rebuild/ folder — the one working
   guide so far is documents/developer-wiki/Needpedia-Test-Site-Package.md
   (see MISSING.txt).

Explain things in plain language. Don't assume the person is technical.
Say what a thing is and what it does before using its name.

WHAT'S NOT IN THIS FOLDER

- Needpedia's database: no users, posts or messages. Your site starts
  empty. The public info pages are in info-resources/, incomplete as
  they are; you put your own versions of them into your site.
- Some content that lived only inside Needpedia's admin pages, such as
  email templates and page pop-up guides. Your new site starts without
  them; write your own.
- Passwords, keys and accounts. None are in here, ever — with one
  known exception noted in MISSING.txt. You make your own.
- rebuild/, the per-system rebuild instructions. Not written yet. Until
  they are, work from inventory.txt and, for the site itself,
  documents/developer-wiki/Needpedia-Test-Site-Package.md.
- The scheduling app. Needpedia had to make its own because other apps
  didn't let people schedule at any time, and Needpedia is too
  international for that. Its code wasn't kept. There are many
  scheduling apps to choose from, and an AI can build a custom one.


THE SYSTEMS, AND A SUGGESTED ORDER

1. Try Needpedia on your own computer first. The test-site package
   (code/needpedia-test-sites) runs copies of the site on any Linux
   computer with Docker and at least 4 GB of memory. You can see it
   working before you spend any money.
2. Make your accounts. The inventory lists them: a rented server, a
   domain name, an email-sending service, a map service (Mapbox), an
   AI account, a website hosting account for the assistants, and a
   Google account for phone notifications.
3. Needpedia itself, on your rented server.
4. Florence (greets visitors) and Lotte (helps logged-in users).
5. Adele and the public email page, where volunteer coordination
   happens in the open.
6. The phone notification app, which tells people when there's
   activity they care about so they come back.
7. The Nexus, a document server for reference articles, AI guides and
   volunteer AI studios.
8. The thread system, a way of working with many AI chats at once, one
   per project. Set up one thread for each part of the system, plus
   one for your own ideal workflow. Tony found it more practical to
   shape his workflow while working in the other threads. Still, you
   never know.

All of Needpedia's AI goes through OpenRouter, one account that reaches
many AI models. You can use a different AI setup; the inventory says
how.


MAKING IT YOUR OWN

- Edit your "about us" section.
- The assistants' knowledge bases are Needpedia's, left as they are.
  They name Needpedia's Patreon, its fiscal sponsor's donation portal
  and Needpedia's email addresses. Put in your own, or take them out.
- The contacts in the inventory are Needpedia's people, not yours.
